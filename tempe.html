About Tempe

What It's Actually Like Living in Tempe, AZ

Tempe's identity is shaped heavily by Arizona State University, whose main campus sits right in the middle of the city. That brings a younger, more transient population than most Valley suburbs, along with a genuinely walkable downtown centered on Mill Avenue and Tempe Town Lake. It's one of the few places in the Valley where you can reasonably live without a car if you choose the right neighborhood.

Outside the immediate downtown and campus area, Tempe has established, family-oriented neighborhoods too, particularly south of the university toward Guadalupe Road. Housing stock ranges from mid-century homes to newer infill development and high-rise condos near the lake.

Moving to Tempe Arizona tends to appeal to buyers who want walkability, proximity to Sky Harbor Airport, and easy access to both Phoenix and the East Valley, along with the energy that comes with a major university being right in town.

Things To Do

Life Outside the Front Door

Tempe Town Lake

A two-mile reservoir with walking paths, kayaking, and regular events.

Mill Avenue District

Restaurants, bars, and shops in Tempe's walkable downtown core.

ASU Sports & Events

Sun Devil Stadium and other campus venues host major sporting and entertainment events.

Papago Park

Shared with Phoenix, home to hiking trails, the Desert Botanical Garden, and the Phoenix Zoo.

Kiwanis Park

One of Tempe's largest parks, with a lake, splash pad, and sports fields.

Light Rail Access

Tempe has some of the best light rail access in the Valley, connecting to Phoenix and Mesa.

An Honest Look

Living in Tempe: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Genuinely walkable downtown, uncommon in the Valley
  • Strong light rail connectivity to Phoenix and Mesa
  • Close to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Energetic, youthful atmosphere from ASU
  • Access to Tempe Town Lake and Papago Park recreation

Things to Consider

  • Housing near campus can mean more renters and turnover in the immediate area
  • Traffic and parking can be difficult near downtown during school events
  • Older housing stock in some neighborhoods needs updating
  • Home prices near the lake and downtown run higher than in South Tempe
  • Noise and activity levels are higher here than in quieter Valley suburbs

New Construction

Building in Tempe

New construction in Tempe is mostly infill, condo and apartment development near downtown and Tempe Town Lake, since the city is largely built out. There's limited large-scale single-family new construction compared to the outer suburbs.

If you're considering a new-build home in Tempe, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving Tempe

Tempe is served by a couple of public school districts, depending on the specific address.

  • Tempe Elementary School District
  • Tempe Union High School District
  • Kyrene School District (parts of South Tempe)

Arizona State University's main campus is located in Tempe, along with a Maricopa Community College campus nearby.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around Tempe

Tempe sits in the central Valley, bordered by Phoenix to the west, Scottsdale to the north, and Mesa to the east.

  • Loop 101 and US-60 both serve the city
  • About 15-20 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 10-15 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Strong light rail access connecting to Phoenix and Mesa
  • Close to Papago Park and Tempe Town Lake

FAQ

Common Questions About Tempe

What is it like living in Tempe, Arizona?

Tempe is an urban, walkable city built around Arizona State University and Tempe Town Lake.

What types of homes are available in Tempe?

A mix of downtown condos, established single-family homes, and some student and investment housing.

Does Tempe have new construction?

Mostly infill condo and apartment development, since the city is largely built out.

How far is Tempe from Downtown Phoenix?

About 15 to 20 minutes.

How far is Tempe from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport?

About 10 to 15 minutes, one of the shortest commutes to the airport in the Valley.

What are some popular neighborhoods in Tempe?

Downtown Tempe near Mill Avenue and South Tempe are both frequently searched.

What is there to do in Tempe?

Tempe Town Lake, Mill Avenue, ASU sporting events, and Papago Park are popular destinations.

What should I know before moving to Tempe?

Areas closer to campus tend to have more renters and activity, while South Tempe offers a quieter, more residential feel.
