About This Home

City living without the chaos.

This is downtown Phoenix the way people actually want to live it. A single-level end unit with a huge front picture window that looks straight into a garden, and south-facing light that drenches the place all afternoon. Mid-century modern, built in 1962, and it has held up beautifully.

Step out the door and you are minutes from Roosevelt Row, Park Central, and a light rail stop that drops you at every concert, every event, and Sky Harbor. You do not need to plan your life around a parking space here.

Big, spacious rooms. A bright kitchen with real storage. A primary suite with a walk-in shower that was recently redone. A private patio for sunrise coffee.

The community itself is the quiet sell. A shared botanical garden courtyard that is genuinely tranquil, a beautiful pool, gated access, and neighbors who have stayed for years, which tells you everything you need to know. Low-maintenance living in a building people do not leave.

If you have been looking for the downtown address without the downtown noise, this is the one. Come see it.

The Neighborhood

Where You'd Be

Park North sits just north of the Encanto district, tucked between Central Avenue and the Clarendon corridor. You are walking distance to Park Central, a short hop down Central to Roosevelt Row, and right beside a light rail stop that makes downtown, the ballpark, the arena and the airport all easy. This is one of the most genuinely walkable pockets in Phoenix.
