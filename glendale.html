About Glendale

What It's Actually Like Living in Glendale, AZ

Glendale punches above its size when it comes to name recognition, largely thanks to State Farm Stadium, home of the Arizona Cardinals and host to Super Bowls and other major events, along with the Westgate Entertainment District that's grown up around it. That area draws visitors from across the Valley for concerts, games, and dining.

Away from Westgate, Glendale has a genuinely historic side too. Catlin Court, in the older downtown area, is known for antique shops and preserved early-1900s bungalows. Most of the rest of the city is made up of established, generally affordable single-family neighborhoods built from the 1960s onward.

Moving to Glendale Arizona tends to appeal to buyers who want a more affordable West Valley option with strong entertainment and sports amenities close by, along with reasonable access to Phoenix.

An Honest Look

Living in Glendale: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Generally more affordable than much of the Valley
  • Major sports and entertainment amenities close to home
  • A genuinely historic, walkable downtown district
  • Good freeway access via Loop 101
  • Established neighborhoods with mature landscaping

Things to Consider

  • Traffic and crowds near Westgate during events and games
  • Some neighborhoods have older housing stock that may need updating
  • Longer commute to the East Valley
  • Noise from stadium events in the closest neighborhoods
  • Less new construction compared to West Valley cities further out

New Construction

Building in Glendale

New construction in Glendale is more limited than in West Valley cities like Surprise or Buckeye, since much of the city is already developed. What exists is concentrated in the northern part of the city and near Westgate.

If you're considering a new-build home in Glendale, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving Glendale

Glendale is served by several public school districts, depending on the specific address.

  • Glendale Union High School District
  • Glendale Elementary School District
  • Peoria Unified School District (parts of northern Glendale)

Glendale Community College operates a campus within the city.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around Glendale

Glendale sits in the West Valley, bordered by Phoenix to the east and Peoria to the north.

  • Loop 101 runs along the western edge of the city
  • About 20-25 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 25-30 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Home to State Farm Stadium and Westgate Entertainment District
  • Close to Thunderbird Conservation Park

FAQ

Common Questions About Glendale

What is it like living in Glendale, Arizona?

Glendale is a family-friendly West Valley city known for State Farm Stadium, Westgate, and a historic downtown district.

What types of homes are available in Glendale?

Mostly established single-family homes, with historic bungalows in Catlin Court and newer construction near Westgate.

Does Glendale have new construction?

Some, mostly in northern Glendale and near Westgate, though less than in West Valley cities further out.

How far is Glendale from Downtown Phoenix?

About 20 to 25 minutes.

How far is Glendale from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport?

About 25 to 30 minutes.

What are some popular neighborhoods in Glendale?

Historic Catlin Court and Arrowhead are among the more searched areas.

What is there to do in Glendale?

State Farm Stadium, the Westgate Entertainment District, and historic Catlin Court are popular destinations.

What should I know before moving to Glendale?

Neighborhoods closest to Westgate can see more traffic and noise during major events, worth considering depending on your preference.
