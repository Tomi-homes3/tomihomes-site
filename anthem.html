About Anthem

What It's Actually Like Living in Anthem, AZ

Anthem is technically an unincorporated community rather than its own city, but it functions like one, built essentially all at once starting in the late 1990s as a large, master-planned development along I-17. That single-developer origin shows in how cohesive the community feels, consistent architecture, extensive shared parks, and a level of amenity investment that's hard to match in an organically grown city.

The community is split into Anthem Country Club, a gated, golf-oriented section, and the broader Parkside area, which makes up most of the community's family-oriented housing. Anthem Community Park anchors the center of town, with pools, sports fields, and regularly scheduled community events throughout the year.

Moving to Anthem Arizona typically appeals to families specifically looking for that master-planned, amenity-heavy lifestyle, in a location that trades some commute time to central Phoenix for a strong sense of community and consistent home quality.

An Honest Look

Living in Anthem: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Consistent, cohesive planning and architecture throughout the community
  • Extensive shared parks and recreational amenities
  • A strong, active sense of community with regular events
  • Both gated golf and non-gated family sections available
  • Generally well-maintained given the community's relatively recent, single-developer origin

Things to Consider

  • Longer commute to central Phoenix and Sky Harbor Airport
  • HOA dues and rules apply throughout the community
  • Limited commercial development within Anthem itself, most major shopping is a drive away
  • Being unincorporated, some municipal services differ from a traditional city
  • Limited housing diversity, since the community was built to a fairly consistent standard

New Construction

Building in Anthem

Anthem is largely built out, so new construction is limited to occasional infill within the existing community footprint, rather than ongoing large-scale development.

If you're considering a new-build home in Anthem, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving Anthem

Anthem is served by a public school district, with schools located within the community itself.

  • Deer Valley Unified School District
  • A number of charter school options in the surrounding area

Paradise Valley Community College's North Valley location is within a reasonable drive.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around Anthem

Anthem sits along I-17 in the North Valley, between Phoenix and Black Canyon City.

  • I-17 provides direct access to the community
  • About 30-35 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 35-40 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Close to New River and Desert Hills for a more rural feel
  • Limited commercial development within Anthem itself
