**What is there to do for Halloween weekend in Phoenix, Arizona?**
Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, and the Valley's fall events are wrapping up with a bang: the Arizona State Fair's final weekend, pumpkin patches, corn mazes, and Halloween night events in downtown Phoenix and Scottsdale.

Halloween on a Saturday? We'll take it. Whether you're in it for the costumes, the candy, or a giant slide at a pumpkin patch, the Phoenix area has plenty going on this weekend.

And if this is your first fall since your move to Phoenix, Arizona, here's a fun one: when the rest of the country turns the clocks back on Sunday, November 1, most of Arizona won't. We don't do daylight saving time. One less thing to worry about after a late night of trick-or-treating.

Here are 10 things to do from Friday, October 30 through Sunday, November 1.

## 1. Catch the last weekend of the Arizona State Fair

The [Arizona State Fair](https://azstatefair.com/events/arizona-state-fair/) wraps up Sunday, November 1, at the State Fairgrounds on McDowell Road in Phoenix. It's open Thursday through Sunday, with gates opening at noon. Rides, fried everything, and one last chance until next year.

## 2. Schnepf Farms Pumpkin & Chili Party, Queen Creek

The [Pumpkin & Chili Party](https://www.pumpkinandchiliparty.com/) wraps up on Halloween. It's open Friday from noon to 10 p.m. and Saturday from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. This year it added five new rides, and favorites like the corn maze, petting zoo, and pig races are back.

## 3. Tolmachoff Farms, Glendale

[Tolmachoff Farms' Pumpkin Days](https://www.tolmachoff-farms.com/post/pumpkin-days-at-tolmachoff-farms-in-glendale-arizona) runs through October 31, with a family corn maze, a mini maze for little ones, a petting zoo, and a big pumpkin patch. Brave enough for more? Its separate AZ Field of Screams haunted maze is open Friday, Saturday, and Halloween night from 7 to 11 p.m.

## 4. Vertuccio Farms Fall Festival, Mesa

The [Vertuccio Farms Fall Festival](https://vertucciofarms.com/events/fall-festival/) runs through October 31 and is open 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. on Friday and Saturday. Admission covers the 5-acre corn maze, the Super Mega Slide, a ropes course, and mini golf. Pumpkins are sold separately.

## 5. Mother Nature's Farm, Gilbert

The [Fall Festival at Mother Nature's Farm](https://mothernaturesfarm.com/fall-festival/) runs through November 1, from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Expect a straw-bale maze, a hayride, and farm animals, and a small pumpkin is included with admission.

## 6. Desert Fall Festival, Surprise

The [Desert Fall Festival](https://tickets.thedesertfallfestival.com/e/fall-festival-2026) runs through November 1. Halloween weekend hours are Friday 5 to 11 p.m., Saturday 4 to 11 p.m., and Sunday 4 to 10 p.m. Highlights include a 90-foot slide, a zipline, and a glowing pumpkin hunt. Tickets are required for ages 3 and up.

## 7. Pumpkin Patch on 303, Goodyear

Looking for something free? The [Pumpkin Patch on 303](https://www.pumpkinpatchon303.com/) runs through October 31 with free admission and free parking, plus a big maze and a scavenger hunt. Weekday hours are 2 to 8 p.m., and weekends are noon to 8 p.m.

## 8. Harvest in the Heritage District, Gilbert

Downtown Gilbert's [Harvest in the Heritage District](https://www.discovergilbert.com/harvest/) wraps up November 1. Its Celebration Soundscape lights up the East Patio at Water Tower Plaza nightly from 6 to 10 p.m., with sound cues at the top of each hour. Pair it with dinner on Gilbert Road and you've got an easy night out.

## 9. PoeFest's "The Raven" at Rosson House, Phoenix

Want spooky without a haunted house? [PoeFest](https://www.poefest.org/) wraps up on Halloween night with performances of Edgar Allan Poe's "The Raven" inside the historic Rosson House Museum in downtown Phoenix. Shows run every 20 minutes from 8 to 10 p.m., and tickets are $20 plus fees.

## 10. FUNHOUSE at W Scottsdale

Night owls, this one's for you. W Scottsdale's [FUNHOUSE Halloween Party](https://event.marriott.com/phxwh-w-scottsdale/events/scottsdale/funhouse--halloween-party-2026) runs Saturday, October 31, from 9 p.m. to 2 a.m. Tickets start at $50 and go up as the night gets closer.

## Before you head out

Event hours, prices, and ticket availability can change, so check each event's website before you go. For more ideas, [GoLocal Arizona's 2026 pumpkin patch guide](https://www.golocalarizona.com/local-arizona-favorites/phoenix-area-pumpkin-patches-corn-mazes-2026-guide) and [Fabulous Arizona's Halloween roundup](https://fabulousarizona.com/arizona-best/halloween-events-attractions-for-families-in-phoenix-2026/) have even more options.

## FAQ

### When does the Arizona State Fair end in 2026?
The 2026 Arizona State Fair runs through Sunday, November 1.

### Does Arizona change its clocks in November?
Most of Arizona doesn't observe daylight saving time, so our clocks stay the same. The Navajo Nation, in northeastern Arizona, is the exception.

### Are there free Halloween weekend events in Phoenix?
Yes. The Pumpkin Patch on 303 in Goodyear has free admission and free parking.
