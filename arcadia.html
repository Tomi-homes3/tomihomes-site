About Arcadia

What It's Actually Like Living in Arcadia, AZ

Arcadia isn't an incorporated city, it's an unincorporated area that straddles part of Phoenix and spills toward Scottsdale, but locally it's treated as its own distinct place. The name comes from the citrus groves that used to cover the area, and while most of those groves are gone, the legacy shows up in the neighborhood's larger lots, mature trees, and irrigation ditches that still run through some properties.

Homes here are mostly single-story ranch-style construction from the 1950s through 1970s, though teardown-and-rebuild has become common as buyers pay a premium for the location and build larger, more modern homes on the same generous lots. The area sits right between Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak, and mountain views are common even without a large budget.

Moving to Arcadia Arizona typically means paying more per square foot than in most of Phoenix, in exchange for lot size, mature landscaping, and a short drive to both Old Town Scottsdale and central Phoenix.

An Honest Look

Living in Arcadia: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Large lots and mature landscaping that are hard to find elsewhere in the Valley
  • Central location between Phoenix and Scottsdale
  • Direct views of Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak in many areas
  • Strong, consistent demand and property values
  • Walkable access to some of the Valley's best-regarded restaurants

Things to Consider

  • Premium pricing per square foot compared to most of Phoenix
  • Many homes are older and require updating or a full rebuild
  • Limited new inventory, since the area is largely built out
  • Not a master-planned community, so no shared HOA amenities
  • Popular hiking trails mean heavier weekend traffic near Camelback Mountain

New Construction

Building in Arcadia

New construction in Arcadia is almost entirely custom, one-off rebuilds on existing lots rather than large-scale development, since the neighborhood is fully built out. Buyers interested in new construction here are typically buying land value and building custom, rather than purchasing from a production builder.

If you're considering a new-build home in Arcadia, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving Arcadia

Arcadia spans parts of a couple of school district boundaries, so which one applies depends on the specific address.

  • Scottsdale Unified School District (eastern Arcadia)
  • Phoenix Union High School District and associated elementary districts (western Arcadia)
  • Several private school options nearby

Arizona State University's Tempe campus is a short drive south of Arcadia.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around Arcadia

Arcadia sits between central Phoenix and Old Town Scottsdale, bordered by Camelback Mountain and Piestewa Peak to the north.

  • No direct freeway through the neighborhood, mostly surface street access via Indian School Road, Camelback Road, and 44th Street
  • About 15-20 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 15-20 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • About 10-15 minutes to Old Town Scottsdale
  • Close to the Biltmore area for shopping and dining
