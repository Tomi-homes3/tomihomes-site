About Chandler

What It's Actually Like Living in Chandler, AZ

Chandler has become one of the Valley's major employment centers, anchored by large tech employers including Intel's manufacturing campus. That economic base has shaped the city's growth, with a steady flow of newer master-planned communities and a downtown that's been deliberately redeveloped into a walkable, restaurant-and-entertainment district.

The Ocotillo neighborhood in south Chandler is known for its network of small lakes and golf courses, offering a different feel from the city's more typical suburban subdivisions. Elsewhere, Chandler is organized much like Gilbert, in planned communities with HOAs, parks, and shared amenities.

Moving to Chandler Arizona often appeals to buyers working in the tech corridor, or families prioritizing strong schools and newer housing within a reasonable commute of both Phoenix and the rest of the East Valley.

An Honest Look

Living in Chandler: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Strong local job market anchored by major tech employers
  • A genuinely walkable, redeveloped downtown district
  • Well-regarded public schools throughout much of the city
  • Distinctive lake and golf communities in Ocotillo
  • Good freeway access via Loop 101 and Loop 202

Things to Consider

  • Home prices run higher than in neighboring Mesa
  • HOAs are common throughout most communities
  • Traffic near the tech corridor can be heavy during commute hours
  • Newer construction is increasingly limited as the city fills in
  • Summer heat is a factor here as it is across the Valley

New Construction

Building in Chandler

New construction in Chandler has slowed as the city has filled in, with what remains concentrated in a few pockets on the city's edges. Buyers looking for significant new-home inventory may find more options in neighboring Gilbert or Queen Creek.

If you're considering a new-build home in Chandler, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving Chandler

Chandler is served primarily by one large public school district, along with some overlap from neighboring districts.

  • Chandler Unified School District
  • Kyrene School District (parts of north Chandler)
  • A number of charter school options throughout the city

Chandler-Gilbert Community College serves the area, with Arizona State University's main campus a short drive north.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around Chandler

Chandler sits in the East Valley, bordered by Tempe to the north, Gilbert to the east, and Ahwatukee to the west.

  • Loop 101 and Loop 202 both serve the city
  • About 25-30 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 20-25 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Home to major tech employers along the Price Road corridor
  • Close to San Tan Village and the Gilbert border

Common Questions About Chandler

What is it like living in Chandler, Arizona?

Chandler is a tech-forward East Valley city with master-planned communities, strong schools, and a walkable downtown.

What types of homes are available in Chandler?

Mostly single-family homes in master-planned communities, plus distinctive lake-and-golf homes in Ocotillo.

Does Chandler have new construction?

Limited, as the city has largely filled in, with more options typically found in neighboring Gilbert or Queen Creek.

How far is Chandler from Downtown Phoenix?

About 25 to 30 minutes.

How far is Chandler from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport?

About 20 to 25 minutes.

What are some popular neighborhoods in Chandler?

Ocotillo, Downtown Chandler, and Fulton Ranch are among the more searched areas.

What is there to do in Chandler?

Downtown Chandler's restaurant scene, golf in Ocotillo, and shopping at San Tan Village.

Home prices run a bit higher than in neighboring Mesa, largely reflecting the strong local job market and schools.
