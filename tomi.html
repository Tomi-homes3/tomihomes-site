In Her Own Words

Thirty years in this Valley, and I still love the drive to a showing.

With over 30 years of experience in Arizona’s residential sales market, I’m here to make your real estate journey as smooth and successful as possible. Whether you’re looking to buy or sell homes in Phoenix, Glendale, Scottsdale, Valley wide or even in Flagstaff, I’m your go-to person.

Most of my business comes from repeat clients and referrals, which means people trust me and keep coming back. When you work with me, you get unparalleled service and deep market insight — an agent who knows how to beat out other offers and sell homes in any kind of market. I got you.

Being a licensed broker (BR103191000) puts me a step above a regular licensed agent, which means you make well-informed decisions that save you time and money. I’m also active on the Arizona Association of Realtors Professional Standards Committee, so you can be sure I hold to the highest industry standards.

With ePro certification and Certified Probate Expert training, I can handle any real estate need you have, including navigating life transitions smoothly. Whether you’re buying a house in Phoenix, selling in Scottsdale, or exploring investment opportunities in Arizona, I’ve got you covered.

My goal is to make your real estate experience smooth, rewarding and even fun. So if you’re ready to buy, ready to sell, or you just need some friendly advice — let’s team up and make it happen. Looking forward to helping you on your real estate journey.
