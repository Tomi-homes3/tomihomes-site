About North Phoenix

What It's Actually Like Living in North Phoenix, AZ

North Phoenix is where the city stops feeling like a historic urban core and starts feeling like the rest of the Valley's newer suburbs. Most of the housing here was built from the 1990s onward, organized into master-planned communities with HOAs, shared parks, and consistent architecture, a very different pattern from the older, more individually built neighborhoods closer to downtown.

Desert Ridge anchors much of the area's identity, with a large outdoor shopping and entertainment center, along with surrounding residential communities. Further north and west, communities like Tatum Ranch and Norterra back directly onto the Sonoran Desert, giving residents quick access to hiking and preserve land without leaving the city.

Moving to North Phoenix typically means a longer commute to downtown in exchange for newer construction, more consistent community amenities, and closer proximity to Scottsdale and the North Valley than you'd get in central or south Phoenix.

An Honest Look

Living in North Phoenix: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Newer housing stock than most of Phoenix
  • Direct access to Sonoran Desert preserve trails
  • Strong retail and entertainment options at Desert Ridge
  • Closer to Scottsdale than central or south Phoenix
  • Consistent, HOA-maintained master-planned communities

Things to Consider

  • Longer commute to downtown Phoenix and Sky Harbor
  • Less walkable than central Phoenix's historic districts
  • Traffic on Loop 101 can be heavy during peak commute times
  • HOA dues and rules apply throughout most communities
  • Fewer historic or architecturally distinct homes

New Construction

Building in North Phoenix

New construction in North Phoenix is more limited than in outer suburbs like Peoria or Surprise, but pockets remain in the far northern part of the area near undeveloped desert land.

If you're considering a new-build home in North Phoenix, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving North Phoenix

North Phoenix is served by a couple of public school districts depending on the specific address.

  • Deer Valley Unified School District
  • Paradise Valley Unified School District (parts of the area)
  • A number of charter school options

Paradise Valley Community College operates a campus in the area.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around North Phoenix

North Phoenix sits north of Bell Road, bordered by Scottsdale to the east and Peoria to the west.

  • Loop 101 runs through the area, providing access to the rest of the North Valley
  • About 25-30 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 30-35 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Close to the Sonoran Preserve for hiking
  • Bordering Cave Creek and North Scottsdale
