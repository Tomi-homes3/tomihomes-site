About This Home

The mountain is your neighbor.

This one sits at the top of Shea, right where the city gives way to the mountain preserve. Look at the aerial photos: the trails start at the end of the block. Hike before work, bike after dinner, and come home to your own pool.

It is a single-level Spanish-style home with real architectural character. Saltillo tile and rich wood floors throughout, a classic beehive fireplace in the separate family room, and French doors that open straight out to the covered patio and pool. The eat-in kitchen has stainless appliances, there is a formal dining room for when you are hosting, and a bathroom was updated in 2024.

No HOA, on a 14,910 square foot lot, with mountain and city light views, an RV gate and a two-car garage. Room to breathe and nobody telling you what color your front door can be.

The neighborhood around it is full of million-dollar homes, and local cafés, restaurants and biking routes are right out the door. You are also an easy drive to downtown and Sky Harbor.

Coming soon. Showings begin October 30. We are holding a public open house October 30 and 31, alongside the estate sale. Want to be first through the door? Get on the list below.
