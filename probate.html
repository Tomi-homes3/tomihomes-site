Public Notice
Please Read: Important

In recent months, we've seen an increase in fraud targeting vacant homes, including cases where a family member has had to leave a property during a probate or other life transition. Criminals notice when a property sits vacant or shows signs of neglect.

They will file a fake deed with the county recorder's office, removing the property from the rightful owner's name and transferring it into their own name or a company's name.

From there, the criminal will attempt to sell the property, which is very possible for them to do. We recommend consulting your attorney right away to help protect against something like this happening to your family. This notice is intended as a courtesy. We always recommend seeking the advice of a qualified attorney, and we're happy to recommend one if you need it.

Do This Today · Free · Two Minutes

Get alerted if anyone tries to sell your property

The Arizona Department of Real Estate runs a free Early Alert System. Register the property and you are notified when a pending sale matches it — while the sale is still pending, before it can close. It is the single fastest step you can take against the fraud described above.
