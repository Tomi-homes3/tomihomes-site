Services Designed Around Your Move

  • Virtual home tours and digital walkthroughs Can’t fly out yet? We walk you through homes live on video, on your schedule, so you can rule places in and out from wherever you are.
  • Market orientation sessions Before you tour anything, we sit down and show you what your money actually buys here, neighborhood by neighborhood, so you are not learning the market on the fly.
  • Connection to local services Utilities, internet, trash, pool service. We hand you the contacts and, where we can, make the call ourselves so the lights are on when you arrive.
  • Neighborhood tours We drive you through the areas you are weighing so you get a firsthand look at the lifestyle, the amenities and the feel of a place before you commit to it.
  • Cultural integration resources Moving from somewhere very different? We connect you with the people and resources that help you settle in and understand how things actually work here.
  • Referrals to trusted local professionals A vetted network of inspectors, lenders, attorneys and contractors, so you are never gambling on a stranger during the most expensive month of your year.
  • Investment consultation If this move is also a purchase decision, we walk through rents, appreciation and holding costs so you know what the numbers look like before you sign.
  • Temporary housing assistance If the timing does not line up, we help you find a short-term rental or a hotel so you are never rushed into the wrong house.
  • Post-move check-in and support We follow up after you have moved in. Questions still come up in month two, and we would rather you call us than google it.
