About South Phoenix

What It's Actually Like Living in South Phoenix, AZ

South Phoenix covers a lot of ground, from the established, self-contained community of Ahwatukee tucked against South Mountain, to Laveen, a rapidly growing area on the western side that's added significant new housing over the past two decades. What ties the area together is South Mountain Park itself, one of the largest municipal parks in the country, which borders much of the region.

Ahwatukee in particular functions almost like its own suburb, separated from the rest of Phoenix by the mountain and reachable mainly via Loop 202. It has its own shopping centers, schools, and a more suburban, cul-de-sac feel than central Phoenix. Laveen, by contrast, is newer and still actively developing, with master-planned communities filling in what used to be agricultural land.

Moving to South Phoenix generally means trading some of central Phoenix's walkability and historic character for more space, newer construction (especially in Laveen), and direct access to South Mountain's hiking and biking trails.

An Honest Look

Living in South Phoenix: What to Weigh

Worth Knowing

  • Direct access to South Mountain Park for hiking and outdoor recreation
  • Generally more affordable than central or north Phoenix
  • Ahwatukee offers a self-contained, suburban feel within city limits
  • Ongoing new construction in Laveen for buyers wanting newer homes
  • Reasonable commute to both downtown Phoenix and Tempe

Things to Consider

  • Ahwatukee's access is limited mainly to Loop 202, which can back up during peak times
  • Laveen is still developing, so retail and amenities are catching up to residential growth
  • Some older sections of South Phoenix have aging infrastructure
  • Less walkable and historically distinct than central Phoenix
  • Summer heat is a factor across the area as it is throughout the Valley

New Construction

Building in South Phoenix

New construction in South Phoenix is concentrated in Laveen, where a significant amount of former agricultural land has been developed into master-planned communities over the past two decades.

If you're considering a new-build home in South Phoenix, you're welcome to have your own real estate representation, even when buying directly from a builder. It's worth thinking about who represents your interests before you visit a model home or register with a builder's sales office.
Schools & Education

School Districts Serving South Phoenix

South Phoenix is served by a couple of public school districts depending on the specific address.

  • Kyrene School District (Ahwatukee)
  • Laveen Elementary School District
  • Phoenix Union High School District
  • A number of charter school options

South Mountain Community College is located within the area.

We're not able to advise on which schools or districts are the "best" fit, since that depends entirely on your family's needs. We're happy to point you toward district boundary maps and third-party research tools like GreatSchools.org so you can look into specific schools yourself.
Location

Getting Around South Phoenix

South Phoenix sits south of Downtown Phoenix, across the Salt River, bordered by Tempe to the east and Laveen to the west.

  • Loop 202 (South Mountain Freeway) serves the area, particularly Ahwatukee and Laveen
  • I-10 provides access to central Phoenix and Tucson
  • About 15-20 minutes to Downtown Phoenix
  • About 15-20 minutes to Sky Harbor Airport
  • Bordered by South Mountain Park
