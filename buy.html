Buyer Roadmap

How this goes, start to keys

Eight steps. No surprises. You will know what is happening and what comes next at every one of them.

01

Find an Agent

Match with an agent who makes you feel comfortable. This is the person you will text at 9pm with a question.

02

Financials

Get your finances in order and get pre-approved, so your offer carries weight the day you write it.

03

Search

Get set up on a personalized search. You see new listings the moment they hit, not three days later.

04

Offer

Make an offer and prepare to negotiate. We go in knowing what the comps actually support.

05

Inspection

Arrange a professional inspection. What it turns up becomes leverage, not a reason to panic.

06

Appraisal

A professional appraiser visits the home and determines value. We know what to do if it comes in low.

07

Schedule Move

Schedule utilities and arrange movers. We hand you the contacts so you are not searching for them.

08

Closing

Get your keys and celebrate your new home. We will be there for that part too.
