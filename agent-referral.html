Why Refer To Us

A referral is a relationship you are lending us

We know what it takes to earn a client, and we know what it costs to lose one. Here is precisely what you and your client can expect once the introduction is made.

01

Support, Whenever Needed

Reach out for any additional resource or hand-hold you need. Our job is to make yours easier, and to make sure your client has everything for a move without friction.

02

Access to Our Expert Network

Movers, handymen, contractors, inspectors, lenders. Decades of vetting, handed over. Your client gets the shortlist instead of the search.

03

Local and Global Expertise

Over 30 years in the Arizona market, with a perspective that reaches beyond it. Wherever your client is coming from, we have met that buyer before.

04

Trust and Clear Communication

Honesty and transparency in every transaction. You will always know where things stand, because we tell you before you have to ask.

05

Enhanced Client Satisfaction

Regular updates to you and to them, through the whole process. Your client should never wonder whether anyone is minding the file.

06

Peace of Mind on Both Sides

Knowing the Arizona end is handled by someone licensed, insured and accountable frees you to do what you do best — close deals and grow your business.

07

Lasting Partnerships

One referral is a transaction. A relationship is a pipeline. We are playing the long game and we would rather you did too.

Partnership Models

Three ways to work together

Take the structure that fits the client and the moment. None of them require you to let go of the relationship.

01

Referral Agreements

A clean referral fee arrangement. You send the client, we handle the Arizona side, you are paid at closing. Nothing complicated.

02

Collaborative Marketing

We co-market the search or the listing so your name stays in front of your client through the entire process, not just the handoff.

03

À La Carte Services

Showings covered, a broker price opinion, someone at the inspection. Take only the pieces you need and keep the rest of the file.
